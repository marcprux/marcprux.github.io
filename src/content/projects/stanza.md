Stanza was the first ebook reader on the iPhone App Store, launching on the opening day of the Apple App Store in 2008. Amazon acquired Lexcycle in April 2009, and by its first anniversary the app had [more than two million downloads](https://press.aboutamazon.com/2009/7/two-million-users-and-12-million-book-downloads-later-stanza-turns-1) (which at the time was an impressive number). I went on to work on Kindle apps. As part of my work on Stanza, I co-authored the [Open Publication Distribution System (OPDS) specification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Publication_Distribution_System) with Hadrien Gardeur.
