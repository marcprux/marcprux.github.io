I worked on Kodo with the founding team at SolarMetric. BEA acquired SolarMetric in 2005, and the core persistence code was donated to Apache as OpenJPA. The [Apache FAQ](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/spaces/openjpa/pages/23625/FAQ) records the project's history.
