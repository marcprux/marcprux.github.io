I'm a software developer in New England. I'm working on [Day](https://daybrite.dev/), a Rust framework for building native apps across phones and desktops.

I also started [the App Fair Project](https://appfair.org/), a non-profit charity based in the United States and France, and serve on [F-Droid's board](https://f-droid.org/2026/04/28/board-appointments-2026.html). Much of my work is about making software easier to build, share, and use.
