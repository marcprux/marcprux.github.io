A reverse-chronological list of public talks, podcast appearances, and
interviews. Where possible, each entry links to a video, abstract, or
recording.

## 2026

- **April 2026** — _"Swift on Android"_ at **iOSoho** (New York City,
  iOSoho's 19th Street offices, in-person; simulcast to NY Android, DC
  Android, DC iOS, and Dallas iOS Developers groups) —
  [recap](https://skip.dev/blog/swift-on-android-iosoho-meetup/) /
  [event](https://www.meetup.com/iosoho/events/314173946/). A walk through
  Swift on Android from the earliest community efforts to the official
  Swift SDK for Android, paired with a Compose Multiplatform talk for a
  "cross-platform doubleheader." (Marc's segment starts at 37:09 of the
  combined recording.)
- **February 2026** — _"Fear and Loathing in the App Stores"_ at **FOSDEM
  2026** (Brussels, main track, in-person) —
  [transcript](https://appfair.org/blog/fear-and-loathing-in-the-app-stores/) /
  [forum thread](https://forum.f-droid.org/t/fosdem-talk-fear-and-loathing-in-the-app-stores/34065).
  Argument for free-software alternatives like F-Droid and AltStore in the
  face of Apple/Google store consolidation and the Android Developer
  Verification program.

## 2025

- **August 2025** — _"People have been working on it for ten years"_
  on **Swift Package Indexing** podcast, episode 61 (~44 min, remote) —
  [episode](https://skip.tools/blog/skip-on-swift-package-indexing-podcast/) /
  [podcast feed](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/swift-package-indexing/id1654567329).
  Swift on Android, the Swift Android Workgroup, and Skip's approach to
  shared SwiftUI codebases.
- **February 19, 2025** — _"The App Fair Project with Marc Prud'hommeaux"_
  on **FSFE Software Freedom Podcast**, episode 30 (recorded in person at
  FOSDEM) —
  [episode](https://fsfe.org/news/podcast/episode-30.en.html) /
  [blog](https://appfair.org/blog/softwarefreedom-podcast-interview).
  The App Fair Project as a universal FOSS app marketplace for iOS and
  Android, and the EU Digital Markets Act.
- **February 12, 2025** — _"Swift on Android with Marc Prud'hommeaux"_
  on **Empower Apps Show** #196 (~56 min, remote) —
  [episode](https://www.empowerapps.show/196) /
  [transcript](https://www.empowerapps.show/196/transcript). Swift on
  Android, Skip.tools, and the road to FOSDEM.
- **February 1, 2025** — _"A Free Software App Store for iOS: the App
  Fair Project's perspective on the DMA"_ at **FOSDEM 2025** (Brussels,
  Legal & Policy track, room H.1301, in-person) —
  [video](https://ftp.belnet.be/mirror/FOSDEM/video/2025/h1301/) /
  [abstract](https://archive.fosdem.org/2025/schedule/event/fosdem-2025-5488-a-free-software-app-store-for-ios-the-app-fair-project-s-perspective-on-the-dma/) /
  [speaker page](https://archive.fosdem.org/2025/schedule/speaker/marc_prudhommeaux/).
  How the EU's Digital Markets Act creates an opening for free-software app
  stores on iOS.
- **January 27, 2025** — _"Abe White and Marc Prud'hommeaux of Skip Tools"_
  on **More Than Just Code** podcast (with Abe White, remote) —
  [episode](https://morethanjustcode.com/abe-white-and-marc-prudhommeaux-of-skip-tools-2/).
  Building cross-platform iOS and Android apps with Skip, Xcode, and
  SwiftUI.

## 2007

- **June 12–14, 2007** — _"An in-depth look at the architecture of an
  object/relational mapper"_ at **ACM SIGMOD 2007** (Beijing, China,
  Industrial Track, "Data persistence and binding" session, in-person) —
  [ACM Digital Library](https://doi.org/10.1145/1247480.1247581) /
  [proceedings index](http://www.sigmod.org/publications/dblp/db/conf/sigmod/sigmod2007.html).
  Industrial-track paper walking through the internals of the Kodo JDO/JPA
  object-relational mapper — caching, query translation,
  persistence-by-reachability, and the trade-offs that show up at scale
  (pages 889–894).

## 2005

- **September 26–30, 2005** — _"EJB3 Persistence API"_ at **JAOO 2005**

  applications.
